How it works

From QR scan to
live scoreboard

01
Athlete registers

Fills out the form, gets a unique ID and QR code with their name baked in.

02
Official scans QR

Points phone at the athlete's QR. Name and ID load instantly — no lookup needed.

03
Results entered

Official logs each attempt. System scores it 0–100 using FITDAT anchor-point math.

04
Scoreboard updates

Composite DCS score appears live. Athletes with all three events rank at the top.
